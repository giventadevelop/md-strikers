Fixture or result card: competition kicker, status tag, two team crests either side of the kick-off time or the scoreline, venue and one CTA.

- `status="upcoming"` shows `time` + `date`, a gold "Next" tag and a primary CTA (tickets).
- `status="live"` shows the score in `display` 40px, the `minute`, and a grana Live tag.
- `status="final"` shows the score and "Full time" with a tertiary CTA (highlights).

The consumer provides team names and, optionally, `crestSrc` images; without them a monogram disc stands in (home in `classic-blue`, away in `grey-700`). This system ships no club crests.
